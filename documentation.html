1. Executive Summary & Core Mission

The primary purpose of this project is the complete digital transformation of dine-in operations: replacing physical paper menus with an interactive, mobile-first QR menu platform.

📱 Instant Guest QR Menu
When guests scan the table QR code, all restaurant offerings appear immediately—including foods, hot/cold drinks, appetizers, desserts, and daily chef specials with real-time stock availability.
👨‍🍳 Admin & Waiter Control (RBAC)
Admins have full control to Add, Edit, Delete, and Hide/Show (Stock Toggle) items. Waiters have dedicated role-based access to the Live Kitchen queue, table service alerts, and stock toggles.

2. Introduction: Transitioning Physical Menus to Digital

2.1 Limitations of Physical Paper Menus

Physical menus suffer from high reprinting costs, physical wear and tear, inability to reflect out-of-stock items, slow table turnarounds, and lack of dietary/allergen transparency. Whenever dishes or prices change, physical menus must be redesigned, printed, and laminated.

2.2 Digital QR Solution & Academic Objectives

This platform eliminates these bottlenecks by providing instant browser-based QR menus with live availability toggling, dietary filters, and direct kitchen order dispatching. The project reinforces computer science coursework in distributed web architecture, role-based security, state synchronization, and human-computer interaction.

5. Admin & Waiter Operational Control (RBAC)

⚙️ Full Admin CRUD & Stock Toggle
Admins can add new dishes/drinks with multiple images, modify prices, delete discontinued items, and toggle items between Active (In-Stock) and Hidden (Out-of-Stock).
👨‍🍳 Waiter / Staff Role Access
Waitstaff are granted dedicated access to the Live Kitchen Orders Queue (/dashboard/orders), table assistance alerts, and item availability toggles on the floor.
🏷️ Printable Acrylic Table Stand PDF
Generates print-ready A5 PDF Table Tents containing the restaurant logo, table number, Wi-Fi credentials, and high-res QR code.
🎨 White-Label Branding Panel
Customize restaurant name, tagline, logo, currency (ETB, USD $, EUR €, GBP £), and 7 color themes with live mobile preview.

8. Challenges Faced & Solutions

Technical Challenge Root Cause Engineering Solution
Vite 5 / Node Toolchain Alignment Alpha devDependencies caused native binding conflicts on Node environments. Realigned all workspaces to stable Vite 5 and standardized root package.json configurations.
Cross-Tab State Sync Admin settings updates did not reflect immediately in open customer tabs. Implemented storage event listeners and custom window.dispatchEvent triggers.
High-DPI PDF QR Printing Canvas QR codes appeared pixelated when rendered into print PDF dimensions. Configured html2canvas with a 3x render scale and high-precision error correction (level="H").
